          About this view

          Explore reads canonical system and object relationships but does not create scientific facts. If a world cannot be visualized, it remains a valid target and the limitation is stated instead of being filled with invented atmosphere, oceans, weather, geology, life, or topography.

          “Another system” follows a bounded site-order navigation rule. It is a product navigation order, not a claim that systems are physically nearest in space.

          Recent destinations, bookmarks, trail position, generated System numbers and the comparison pin are local product state. They never become part of the astronomical record.
