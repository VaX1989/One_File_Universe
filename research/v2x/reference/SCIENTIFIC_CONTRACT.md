# V2X-16 scientific and authority contract

**RESEARCH ONLY.** Nothing under `research/v2x/**` is canonical world truth, shipping registration, measured runtime evidence, central P4 authority, or permission to mutate canonical state.

## Exact within the declared model

Integer stock-flow conservation, cap enforcement, cohort balancing, graph acyclicity, reaction elemental balance, simultaneous compartment transfer conservation, and declared bounded-allocation rules are formal properties of this research representation. Exactness here means exactness of the representation, not empirical truth.

Array order is not silently granted scientific authority. Propagule routing, logistics contention, transport edges, and microstructure interface contention allocate from an opening state with deterministic bounded reconciliation. Reaction networks intentionally remain sequential operator-splitting scenario models and explicitly make no physical permutation-invariance claim.

## Identity and determinism

Deterministic research addresses and state fingerprints are **not canonical identity**. They use bounded, length-prefixed fields and a noncryptographic 64-bit fingerprint to reduce accidental aliasing. A fingerprint match is only a lookup hint: metadata/state must be compared before treating two research objects as equivalent. No collision-resistance or security claim is made.

## Life non-claims

Thermodynamic opportunity never implies life. `SOURCE_BACKED` opportunity requires explicit source id/reference/version. There is no abiogenesis probability, universal alien biochemistry, universal metabolic exponent, or universal species threshold. Resource/energy quanta are abstract, not joules or moles. Selection/speciation/succession parameters are scenario inputs unless separately calibrated.

## Individuals non-claims

Cohort transitions are accounting over supplied rates, not real population forecasts. Materialized persons are ephemeral bounded refinements with no canonical person identity, psychology, or predicted biography. Their request/state fingerprint prevents stale aggregate reconciliation; mixed request batches and forged ID/fingerprint/slot metadata fail closed, without creating persistent person identity. Exact genealogy is deliberately lost outside the retained kinship window.

## Civilization non-claims

Production/logistics are bounded stock-flow abstractions, not price equilibrium or welfare economics. Technology DAGs do not predict invention dates. Knowledge transmission is a non-conservative information-copy proxy and saturation is explicitly witnessed. Coordination/conflict/collapse fields are not universal sociology. Convention state does not predict grammar, ethnicity, belief, or psychology. Archaeological traces require bounded explicit modeled causal origin.

## Matter non-claims

Stoichiometric balance is formal; per-tick reaction extent is not a physical rate law without source-backed kinetics, units, state, and validity. Transport is a simultaneous bounded scenario operator, not a diffusion/advection law. Microstructure transfer is a representative bounded operator, not a constitutive law. Atom/bond structures are bounded representations, not trajectories or exact bulk inventory. Source-backed and computed structures require explicit source/version/method provenance. Computed structures cannot be promoted to experimental authority. Unknown bond order remains unknown.

The regime classifier routes model-family requirements only. It never solves electronic/nuclear quantum dynamics and never emits electron orbits, exact electron positions, wavefunctions, or tunneling amplitudes. `pseudoQuantumVisualsAllowed=false` is invariant.

## Cross-domain authority

Cross-domain fixtures are falsification oracles/proposals only. They cannot admit events or mutate canonical environment/history. Promotion must pass convergence/P4/cross-scale authority review.
