# R&D-15 Astronomy / Cosmic Depth Research Report

## Result

Five bounded research surfaces were advanced while preserving frozen P2/P3/P4 authority: deterministic morphology posterior; stellar-population/lifetime abstraction; mass-dependent multi-star period/q/e candidate generator; six-sample local 3D gradient over the existing P3 synthetic density field; and observer-query observability proxy.

The morphology, population/evolution and multiplicity models are **canonical-successor candidates only**. They require calibration, uncertainty models, new semantic-manifest/versioning, migration and central promotion. The spatial-gradient and observability models are **model-derived/presentation-query options only** under current evidence.

## Identity / state compatibility

The provider accepts existing P3 keys and calls frozen P3 resolvers. It never generates replacement EntityIdentity values. It returns the original P3 entity under `canonical` and the same ID under `sourceEntityId`. Research random draws are property-separated P2 derives using the canonical P3 address and a research-only versioned domain. P4 is not imported and no event/mutation is emitted.

## Scientific maturity

- Morphology: empirical direction, stylized coefficients; not fitted to GAMA/Galaxy Zoo.
- Stellar evolution: deterministic bookkeeping over low-fidelity P3 facts; detailed successors should use admitted MIST-equivalent grids with chemistry/uncertainty dimensions.
- Multiplicity: empirical direction, coarse bins; not Moe-Di Stefano's fitted joint likelihood and not dynamically integrated.
- Spatial prior: hypothetical/model-derived only; not measured filament orientation or physical coordinates.
- Observability: inverse-square/parallax terms are formal geometry; the final score is stylized and not a survey selection function.

## Determinism / resources

Executable model arithmetic is integer BigInt with Q16/Q32 scaling. No floating-point RNG, transcendental sampling, locale, clock or global catalogue is used. Work is per addressed entity: exactly six neighboring density calls for spatial gradient and at most four stars / three companion candidates per frozen P3 system. Independent Python integer oracle fixtures cover selected pure relationships.
