# V1X-17 Research Report — Life / Ecology / Evolution

## Result

V1X-17 advances positive biology to a bounded, deterministic, replay-testable **MODEL_DERIVED_SIMULATION** research prototype without modifying canonical P6. It is a successor-design experiment, not a promotion claim.

Implemented blocks: explicit environment readiness; integer/ppm population ecology; stage lifecycle conservation; P4-bound event identity/replay; deterministic bounded mutation proposal; criterion-bound speciation; exact-zero demographic extinction with lineage persistence; REFINE/PROJECT/RECONCILE aggregate-to-organism sampling; biology-to-environment feedback proposal; and independent Node/Python deterministic witnesses.

## Scientific boundary

**FORMAL:** integer/ppm bounds, count conservation, canonical serialization/hash identities, P4-key-bound replay, exact-zero demographic extinction inside represented state, bounded active set, stale-refinement rejection.

**APPROXIMATE / EMPIRICAL EARTH:** stage-structured demography, energetic/nutrient/redox constraints, trophic/dispersal relationships and biosphere-environment feedback as scientifically motivated abstractions. Parameters are explicit inputs rather than hidden constants.

**STYLIZED / HYPOTHETICAL:** carrying-capacity suppression, synthetic integer traits, hash-driven mutation opportunities and all positive post-genesis seed fixtures.

No result establishes life on an OFU planet, predicts abiogenesis, proves habitability, supplies canonical taxonomy, predicts alien morphology or establishes a universal ecological law.

## Adversarial decisions

1. Rejected a fixed trophic-efficiency rule; efficiency remains edge-specific input.
2. Network topology has no stability/specialization/coextinction authority.
3. Bulk element presence is not nutrient availability; requirements are profile-specific and bioavailable.
4. Rejected a universal maintenance-power threshold.
5. Speciation requires an explicit criterion witness; no universal trait-distance/branching-rate threshold.
6. Mutation proposals cannot infer molecular genetics or macroevolution.
7. Fine organism samples are ephemeral and cannot override aggregate state.
8. Environment feedback is a proposal to upstream authority, never private canonical mutation.
