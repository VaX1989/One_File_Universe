# Maintainer Ladder

OFU uses earned, scoped trust.

## Contributor

Open to everyone. No prior permission required to propose work.

## Trusted Contributor

Typical evidence: multiple useful contributions or reviews, reliable communication, and demonstrated respect for project contracts.

Possible privileges: issue triage, labels, mentoring, non-binding review.

## Reviewer

Demonstrates repeated ability to find defects, reason about authority/evidence, and review constructively in one or more areas.

## Domain Maintainer

Demonstrates sustained ownership of an area: design judgment, review quality, regressions, contributor support, and compatibility stewardship.

## Core Maintainer

Demonstrates cross-domain architectural judgment and deep knowledge of canonical/runtime/build/governance boundaries.

## Integration Maintainer

Demonstrates reliable exact-SHA integration, conflict adjudication, evidence discipline, and willingness to stop a promotion rather than weaken a gate.

## TSC

Selected from maintainers with broad trust and demonstrated project-level judgment. TSC membership is governance responsibility, not a reward or permanent title.

## Principles

- No role is earned by commit count alone.
- Employment or sponsorship does not purchase privilege.
- People may hold different levels in different areas.
- Review and mentoring count as first-class contributions.
- Inactive roles can be made emeritus without erasing past contribution.
- The project should grow maintainers proactively before an area becomes a bottleneck.
