# AI-Assisted Contributions

AI-assisted contributions are welcome. They are not exempt from normal responsibility.

## Contributor responsibility

The human/organization submitting a contribution is responsible for correctness, security, licensing, provenance, scientific claims, tests, and review responses regardless of whether AI tools helped produce the work.

## Disclosure

Material AI assistance should be disclosed in a pull request when it affects reviewer risk assessment—for example substantial generated code, scientific derivation, generated assets/data, or large automated refactors. Routine autocomplete need not produce a diary of every suggestion.

## Prohibited shortcuts

Do not use an AI system's statement as scientific evidence, licensing clearance, security proof, or test execution proof. Do not submit generated material when you cannot reasonably establish the right to contribute it.

## Review

Reviewers evaluate AI-assisted code by the same contracts and evidence as human-written code. Generated volume is not a reason to lower review standards; large generated changes may be required to split or supply stronger machine-checkable evidence.
