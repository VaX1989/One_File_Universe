# IP and Data Provenance

OFU treats legal provenance like scientific provenance: absence of evidence is not converted into authority.

The historical pre-community corpus must be audited before the project represents that every byte can be commercially relicensed under a single chain of title. This does not deny existing authorship; it prevents an overbroad legal claim before third-party code, data, assets and material AI-assisted contributions have been classified.

New third-party material should receive a provenance record conforming to `config/governance/ip-provenance.schema.json` before merge. Embedded datasets require explicit redistribution rights because the canonical product physically packages required baseline material into one offline artifact.

The project targets REUSE Specification 3.3 / SPDX-style file licensing after the historical corpus audit. Until coverage is real, OFU must not display a REUSE-compliant badge or claim complete machine-readable copyright coverage.

This policy is operational provenance, not legal advice. Final dual-licensing and contributor-rights language still requires qualified software/IP counsel.
