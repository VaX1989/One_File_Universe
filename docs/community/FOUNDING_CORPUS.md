# Founding Corpus Boundary

Before OFU begins large-scale external contribution, the project records an exact **pre-community-scale corpus boundary** in `config/governance/founding-corpus.json`.

The boundary is the development tree at:

- repository: `VaX1989/One_File_Universe`
- commit: `ae406dbc8ac64fbd9161566ac1367bfacf2496f9`
- tree: `e62971a10f3f54912197bc3904ac2f64e6c525cc`
- recorded date: September 8, 2026

This is useful because future contributions can be separated chronologically from the corpus that existed before the community-scale licensing/governance transaction. It strengthens provenance, contributor-rights bookkeeping and future chain-of-title audits.

## What the record does not claim

The record is **not** a declaration that every byte in the historical corpus has already been proven to have one exclusive copyright owner. OFU has been developed with a mixture of original engineering, external technical references, scientific data/models and AI-assisted workflows; third-party and AI-assisted material must be classified rather than erased from the provenance story.

Accordingly, the machine-readable record explicitly states:

- exclusive copyright ownership of every path: `NOT_ASSERTED_PENDING_IP_AUDIT`;
- complete commercial-relicensing chain of title: `NOT_ASSERTED_PENDING_IP_AUDIT`;
- audit status: `IP_AND_PROVENANCE_AUDIT_REQUIRED`.

That is deliberate. A precise boundary plus an honest audit state is stronger than a sweeping ownership statement that has never been verified.

## Relationship to v1.0.0

The same record also pins the published historical v1.0.0 release identity (commit, tree, asset size and SHA-256). The historical release remains evidence of what was shipped; the founding-corpus boundary identifies the later pre-community development state from which the Open Source/community architecture is being established.

## Future contributors

Once a reviewed contributor-rights process is active, new copyright-significant contributions should be traceable to their authors and accepted rights records. The founding boundary therefore creates a clean chronological seam:

`pre-community corpus` → `community-scale governance activation` → `externally contributed corpus with explicit provenance`.
