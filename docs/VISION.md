# One File Universe — Founder Vision

**Status:** long-range product destination; capability claims remain governed by current versioned contracts and evidence.

## One file. One universe. Verifiable by construction.

One File Universe is intended to become a deterministic, causal, persistent, mutable and deeply explorable procedural universe contained in one self-contained HTML artifact. The player should be able to move freely across supported reality—from cosmic structure toward worlds, arbitrary supported surface locations, living systems, history and future microscopic regimes—while the underlying universe remains coherent, inspectable, reproducible and bounded in materialized runtime cost.

OFU is not intended to be a planet viewer, scientific dashboard, curated-destination demo, procedural-noise showcase, fixed sequence of scene buttons, or engineering tool with a renderer attached.

The project is simultaneously:

1. a playable procedural universe;
2. a deterministic world-generation and simulation reference architecture;
3. a browser systems-engineering experiment;
4. a reproducibility, scientific-authority and conformance experiment;
5. a demonstration that a very deep application can remain one portable artifact.

## The experiential promise

A mature OFU should let a user:

- observe and choose among many supported galaxies rather than one showcase galaxy;
- enter arbitrary supported galactic regions, neighborhoods and stellar systems;
- select supported stars, planets and moons directly;
- orbit, approach, descend and reverse direction without losing entity continuity;
- choose supported surface positions spatially rather than only predetermined local patches;
- explore, revisit and compare locations in the same world lineage;
- encounter geography constrained by planetary structure rather than unrelated local noise;
- encounter environments, ecosystems, organisms, settlements, cultures and histories only where their upstream authority exists;
- interact with the world through governed events and later observe persistent consequences;
- progressively inspect smaller biological structures as future model regimes become available;
- reach the exact evidence, provenance, addresses, manifests and replay state behind what is shown.

This is **UNIVERSAL_EXPLORATION_FREEDOM**: address/query-driven freedom within supported model domains, not a promise that every conceivable point or scale is already implemented.

## A deep but defensible multiscale universe

The target semantic ladder can extend through:

`Universe → cosmic structure → galaxy → galactic region → sector → stellar neighborhood → stellar system → star → planet/moon → orbit → atmosphere/approach → global surface → continental/planetary region → regional geography → local geography → human scale → ecosystem → organism → organ/tissue → microbial scale → cell → subcellular structure → molecular regime → atomic regime → future non-classical regimes`.

This is not one naive Euclidean containment tree. `ModelRegime`, resolution and fidelity are separate concepts. A molecular or future quantum-compatible representation may use semantics that are not a smaller classical scene. The architecture reserves explicit cross-regime contracts without claiming brute-force quantum simulation.

## Cross-scale continuity

The target is constrained progressive refinement, not scene substitution.

- planetary geography constrains regional geography;
- regional geography constrains local terrain;
- climate constrains biome eligibility;
- biomes constrain ecosystems;
- ecosystems constrain organisms;
- organism/anatomy state constrains cellular materialization;
- civilization and history constrain settlements, artifacts and ruins;
- important local consequences can be projected upward.

`REFINE` produces finer detail under committed constraints. `PROJECT` aggregates significant fine consequences into coarser state. `RECONCILE` validates that representations agree on declared invariants. Presentation can interpolate or decorate; it cannot silently become authority.

## Planetary reality target

Current P5 canonical terrain topology and Environment v2 are valuable foundations, not final planetology. Future versioned planetary models may establish, where scientifically supportable, bulk composition, interiors, differentiation, geodynamics, tectonic regimes or appropriate alternatives, crustal provinces, volcanism, impacts, cratering, faulting, orogeny, basins, sediments, weathering, erosion, hydrological networks, coastlines, oceans, ice, soils/regolith, atmospheres, clouds, climate transport, climate zones, seasonality and local weather.

Each promoted model must declare authority, evidence class, model fidelity, assumptions, canonical inputs and outputs, invariants/conservation laws, dependency graph, cost model and conformance evidence. Presentation noise never becomes geology by visual plausibility alone.

## Life, evolution and ecology target

P6 v1 is scientifically honest precisely because it fails closed on the current real Environment v2 path. The long-range destination nevertheless includes versioned models that may support positive biological worlds when upstream conditions and scientific authority justify them.

Future biology may include microbial ecosystems, producers, consumers, decomposers, trophic networks, competition, symbiosis, mutation, selection, adaptation, population dynamics, speciation, extinction, developmental stages, aquatic/terrestrial organisms, plants or functional analogues, animals or functional analogues, arthropod-like or alien analogues, and intelligent species.

Earth taxonomy is not universal law. Every biological model must distinguish established/general constraints, Earth-calibrated empirical knowledge, hypothetical exobiology and generative fictional extensions. Abiogenesis eligibility is a research frontier and must not be invented merely to make a planet feel alive.

## Civilization, culture and history target

Civilization is not a Boolean decoration. Settlements and societies should ultimately be explainable consequences of environment, resources, biology, population, technology, interaction and prior history.

Future versioned domains may cover populations, settlements, cities, infrastructure, technology, production, resources, trade, economies, institutions, governance, factions, diplomacy, conflict, migration, language, culture, beliefs, knowledge, art, artifacts, ruins, archaeology and ecological/planetary consequences.

A ruin should be evidence of a prior causal history, not a prop generated after the player arrives.

## Late materialization as an enabling technology

OFU cannot and should not simulate every person, cell or molecule at all times. The COLD/WARM/HOT/IMMEDIATE philosophy is central:

- **COLD:** constrained statistical/macroscopic state;
- **WARM:** active populations, ecosystems, economies or regions;
- **HOT:** local settlements, ecological systems, groups and concrete processes;
- **IMMEDIATE:** entities directly involved in observation or interaction.

Finer representations are materialized from committed constraints. Significant local effects can `PROJECT` upward. This keeps universe depth compatible with finite memory and compute.

## Player intervention

Future gameplay may permit environmental change, construction, resource movement, terraforming, biological seeding or other interventions. Any action that changes world truth must be expressed through versioned canonical events and domain transition semantics under temporal authority. A UI shortcut, renderer state or developer console must never directly rewrite canonical facts.

## Graphics as a co-equal pillar

The target is maximum scientifically honest visual quality the browser and available hardware can sustain without changing canonical truth. Long-range rendering may include robust astronomy, stellar appearance, planetary-class differentiation, physically inspired materials, atmosphere scattering, supported clouds/water/ice, terrain materials, geological visual structure, vegetation and organisms where authorized, cities and artificial lighting, shadows, volumetrics, particles, adaptive LOD, instancing, streaming/virtualization, temporal anti-aliasing or alternatives, upscaling, dynamic resolution and hardware quality profiles.

Strict portability retains a WebGL2-class baseline. The same artifact may expose an optional WebGPU Enhanced path. GPU output remains presentation/derived state unless an explicit future canonical conformance contract proves otherwise.

## UX as a co-equal pillar

The long-range hierarchy is **UNIVERSE FIRST → CONTEXT SECOND → TECHNICAL EVIDENCE THIRD**.

- **Explore:** viewport-dominant direct manipulation; look, point, select, zoom, travel, approach, land, move, observe, interact, return.
- **Inspect:** contextual scientific understanding—what the object is, what is known/derived, uncertainty, scientific status and comparisons.
- **Lab:** raw canonical address and identity, manifests, digests, provenance, replay, archives, performance, renderer internals and certification evidence.

Explore/Inspect/Lab is conceptual information architecture, not a requirement that the final product be panel-centric. Menu/stage controls may remain as accessibility anchors, fast travel and debugging, while direct object picking, tap/drag, orbit, wheel/pinch zoom, spatial target selection and reverse traversal become primary.

Mobile is a first-class target: touch ownership, pinch and drag, tap-vs-drag discrimination, safe areas, contextual bottom sheets, readable context, keyboard alternatives, reduced motion, screen-reader semantics, accessible target sizes and non-color-only communication. Quality may scale by hardware; canonical truth does not.

## Artifact-size philosophy

The goal is not the smallest HTML. The canonical distribution remains one self-contained HTML artifact. It may grow substantially when bytes represent useful governed capability, reference data, assets, shaders, diagnostics or conformance material. Artificial padding, duplicate unreachable payload and meaningless inflation remain forbidden.

`artifact size != runtime working set`.

The optimization target is maximum useful universe depth, quality and capability per acceptable runtime cost—not minimum distribution bytes.

## What OFU does not claim today

This vision does not claim that current OFU has real geology, canonical oceans, greenhouse surface climate, working biospheres, civilizations, microscopic simulation, molecular dynamics or quantum mechanics. It does not claim literal infinite detail. Every implemented regime remains finite, versioned and declared.

## Definition of future success

OFU succeeds when a player can meaningfully choose where to go, experience continuity across scales, encounter consequences that can be explained by the universe's causal state, change that state through governed events, return to it later, and inspect the evidence behind it—while the artifact remains portable, bounded in active working set, performant enough to explore, visually compelling, accessible and independently verifiable.
