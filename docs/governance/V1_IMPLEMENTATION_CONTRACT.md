## Authority and scientific honesty

`CANONICAL_PROVEN` names only the applicable frozen/promoted evidence-backed
contract. `MODEL_DERIVED_SIMULATION` is an authorized shipping class for explicit
causal models, including hypothetical exobiology and societies. It requires a
versioned model, known inputs, applicability, assumptions, determinism, evidence
and fidelity; it is not permission to present invented measurements as science.
`PRESENTATION_ONLY` covers visual/audio approximation and must not mutate either
canonical facts or model state. Research prototypes do not gain authority from
being copied into a build.

P6 v1 remains fail-closed on current canonical Environment v2 evidence. A new
model-derived biosphere can exist without falsifying that result; its UI and
provenance must identify the separate model lineage. Physical terrain, climate,
geology, abiogenesis, alien biochemistry and cultural outcomes must disclose
model limits. Source-backed atomic properties must be distinguished from
synthetic atomic representations. Bounded microscopic visualization is not a
claim of quantum simulation or experimentally validated molecular dynamics.
Non-classical research stays explicitly reserved beyond the supported models.

## Acceptance and release

Quality is conjunctive: semantic correctness, scientific honesty, direct freedom,
causal depth, continuity, visual coherence, UX/accessibility, persistence,
performance, offline portability and reproducibility all matter. Green aggregate
CI cannot excuse a material visible defect, an empty primary capability or a
misleading scientific claim. Test repairs must prove the intended invariant and
preserve or strengthen it; no browser or required assertion is removed to pass.

Final certification binds one exact source/tree and actual shipping artifact to
all frozen upstream checks, new contract/model/adversarial tests, five required
browser platforms, direct-file/offline/no-network, complete product journeys,
responsive compact/mobile/landscape, context recovery where measurable,
repeated navigation/cache/persistence/resource campaigns and independent
cross-scale witnesses. Build twice must reproduce the same bytes. Distribution
size, startup, working memory and timing are measured separately; useful payload
is permitted and artificial inflation is not.

Physical Android/iOS remain `NOT_VERIFIED` unless actual device evidence is
obtained. That label is not a prohibition on continued engineering or normal
release under this mandate. Browser emulation, injected input, CPU models and
fallback rendering must state their methods and never masquerade as physical
or GPU measurements. Release completion requires reading back the published tag,
source identity and downloaded attachment and verifying its size and digest.
